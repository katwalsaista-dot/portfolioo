About me

      

        

          
I’m a second-year student studying Cybersecurity at Softwarica College. I’m focused on ethical hacking, web application security and networking, and I practice through labs, CTFs and small projects.

          
I chose cybersecurity because I’m curious about how systems work and how they can be broken. Finding a weakness and helping fix it before someone misuses it feels like solving a puzzle that protects real people.

          
I’m looking for an internship or entry-level role in penetration testing, SOC analysis or application security. I’d also love a mentor who can review my write-ups and show me how security work happens in a real team.
